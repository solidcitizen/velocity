## Profiles

Scale the record to risk. The fields above are the **Standard** profile.

- **Minimal signal** — lightweight capture, low-risk work, or early triage: Status, Environment, operator sentence or observed problem, Facts, Unknowns, next owner or step.
- **Standard** — normal product delivery: the fields in this template.
- **Full trust record** — high-risk, cross-system, incident, production, security, data, or operator-trust work: all Standard fields plus Lane and Mode, Proof class required, Proof mapping, Branch disposition, Authority owner, and related tranches / ADRs / review packs / promotion records.

The project overlay says which profile is the default and when to escalate.
