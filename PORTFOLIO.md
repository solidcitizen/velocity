## VEL-PF-2 — Strategic goal-setting

- Accountable owner: Velocity Maintainer.
- Outcome sought: a governed entity can establish, compare, adopt, evaluate, and revise goals,
  with AI assistance, explicit values/constraints, accountable authority, and evidence of benefit.
- Strategic relationship: extends Velocity from carrying out accepted direction to helping
  formulate direction. This is the future capability the Maintainer identified on 2026-09-21.
- Posture: **candidate — future, uncommitted**.
- Decision status/authority: **proposed**. Recording this opportunity is authorized; selecting
  or executing a goal-setting initiative is not authorized by this entry.
- Stage/evidence: captured opportunity. No strategic-goal method, automated-goal runtime,
  business case, resource estimate, or realized-benefit result is claimed.
- Authorized scope/capacity: none for execution. No budget, staffing, delivery date, or automatic
  scheduled start. Existing authority permits maintaining this record.
- Questions for a possible discovery brief: how to elicit values and competing objectives;
  who may adopt/change goals; how to compare alternatives and uncertainty; what constitutes
  outcome evidence; how to challenge/revise a goal without an agent rewriting its own criteria.
  Compare the full PMI AI standard and other relevant practice before claiming an uncovered
  AI-entity lifecycle or goal-setting capability; the current review covers public sources only.
- Options/tradeoffs: keep manual goal ownership with AI evidence support; investigate bounded
  delegated goal formation; or defer. No option is selected here.
- Next decision/owner/trigger: at the Maintainer's next portfolio prioritization, or an explicit
  request to take this up, decide whether to charter bounded discovery. Its charter would name
  decision rights, exclusions, capacity/time limits, research questions, proof, and a review gate.
  Capture does not automatically create that charter or enter a task in the execution queue.
- Dependencies: use the authority/context distinctions from `VEL-PF-1`; reconcile with the
  separately owned [Entity Development Lifecycle proposal](https://github.com/solidcitizen/velocity/pull/11)
  before any overlapping design is accepted. Neither dependency implies automatic activation.
- History: 2026-09-21, Coordinator captured the Maintainer's explicitly requested future
  portfolio opportunity. It remains unselected until a new authoritative decision is recorded.
